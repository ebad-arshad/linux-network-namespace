Linux Networking Namespace: Network namespaces in Linux provide a powerful form of virtualization, allowing the creation of isolated network environments within a single host. These namespaces are essential for containerization, network testing, and security segmentation, making them a cornerstone of modern infrastructure management. They provide the capability to encapsulate network devices, interfaces, routing tables, and firewall rules, and offering a robust framework for building complex networking solutions.
